HydroSeq Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

HydroSeq is developed by Seth Parsons. Contact sethparsons6@icloud.com with privacy questions.

Data you enter

HydroSeq saves your projects, plant names, component libraries, engineering inputs, and machine sequences on your device. Calculations run on your device. The app has no HydroSeq account, advertising, or third-party analytics. The developer does not receive your projects or library data through ordinary app use.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, HydroSeq syncs eligible projects, libraries, and location records across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple CloudKit stores these records in your private iCloud database. HydroSeq stores an iCloud account identifier on your device to detect account changes. Apple's privacy policy applies to its services.

Exporting, sharing, and deleting data

You choose which data to export and where to save or share it. A file may include project details, equipment specifications, or plant names. Manage or delete exported files in the service where you saved them. Deleting the app from a device does not automatically remove copies in iCloud or files you exported.

Support and this website

Contacting the developer from the app opens an email draft with the HydroSeq version and build. You choose whether to send it and what else to include. The developer uses information you send to respond to your request.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub handles its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
