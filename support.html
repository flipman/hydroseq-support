HydroSeq Support

For help with calculations, project files, or iCloud sync, email sethparsons6@icloud.com. In the app, you can also open Settings → Contact Developer.

Include your HydroSeq version, device and OS version, and the steps that led to the issue. For a calculation question, include the inputs and their units. Use sample values or remove workplace and equipment identifiers before sharing files or screenshots.

Backups and sync

Open Settings → Backup & Sync to check iCloud status and find backup and restore options. To sync across iPhone and iPad, use the same Apple Account on both devices.
